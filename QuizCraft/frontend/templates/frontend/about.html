About QuizCraft

QuizCraft is an adaptive online quiz platform built as an academic Python/Django project. It selects question difficulty in real time based on student performance, tracks every attempt in MongoDB, and provides both students and faculty with meaningful, data-driven analytics — no hardcoded numbers, no fake charts.

Our Goal

Give every student a quiz that matches their current skill level instead of a one-size-fits-all test.

Tech Stack

Python, Django, Django REST Framework, MongoDB (PyMongo), HTML5/CSS3, JavaScript, Chart.js.
